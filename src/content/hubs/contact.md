faqs:
  - question: Is the consultation really free?
    answer: >-
      Yes. It is usually a phone call: tell us what is going on and where, and most problems can be
      identified on that call. There is no charge and no obligation, and if we are not the right fit
      for the job, we will tell you before anyone books a visit.
  - question: How is the work priced after the consultation?
    answer: >-
      It depends on the kind of work. Routine work, meaning general pest control and quarterly or
      monthly maintenance programs, is priced off a standard schedule and can be quoted over the phone.
      Custom work, such as structural exclusion, rodent work on a building, bed bugs and multi-unit or
      commercial programs, starts with a paid site inspection.
  - question: Why do you charge for an inspection on custom work?
    answer: >-
      Because the inspection is real work rather than a sales visit. We go through the crawlspace or
      cellar, the roofline, the envelope and the penetrations, then give you written findings and a
      scope of work you can act on, including any repair that belongs to another trade. The fee is
      quoted before the visit and comes off the cost of the job if you hire us.
  - question: What should I include when I describe the issue?
    answer: >-
      Whatever you have noticed, in your own words: what you are seeing or hearing, where in the
      building, how long it has been going on, and roughly what kind of building it is. You do not
      need to know the species, because identifying it is our job. Mention any earlier treatment, or an
      audit or violation behind the call.
  - question: What happens after I send the form?
    answer: >-
      It goes directly to the business email and is read by a person, with a reply within one business
      day and usually sooner. The form does not ask for your street address, because that only matters
      once an inspection is being scheduled. If it is urgent, call (631) 212-9601 instead.
  - question: Is the phone really answered 24 hours a day?
    answer: >-
      Yes. A person answers at any hour, including weekends. That does not mean a truck at every hour:
      an overnight call gets a diagnosis and a schedule. Some work is done after dark on purpose, such
      as a restaurant kitchen once it has closed, or a hornet nest while the colony is inside.
  - question: How do commercial and building inquiries work?
    answer: >-
      Say on the form that it is commercial and what the site is, because that changes who calls you
      back. For a co-op or condo, the first call goes best with the managing agent and the
      superintendent on the line. For a food facility, have the audit scheme and the next audit date
      ready.
  - question: How can I check your licenses before hiring?
    answer: >-
      Verify them with the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation rather than
      trusting any website, including this one. Graduate Pest Control holds pesticide business
      registration 03298, and Ryan Katz holds certified applicator license C1822141 in categories 7A,
      7F and 8.
